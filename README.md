# Periodus

> **A thoughtful, distraction-free desktop EPUB reader crafted for deep reading and comprehension.**

Periodus re-imagines digital reading by breaking long-form texts into intentional, paragraph-by-paragraph reading journeys. Instead of endless vertical walls of text, Periodus helps you absorb every concept, thought, and narrative rhythm with deep focus, ambient visual warmth, and contextual assistance.

---

## 🌟 The Reading Experience

### 📖 Focused Paragraph-by-Paragraph Immersion
- **Micro-Reading Cadence**: Read books one paragraph at a time to reduce cognitive fatigue and elevate comprehension.
- **Rich Typography & Formatting**: Full Markdown and typography parsing powered by *Literata*, *Newsreader*, and *Fraunces*—rendering author quotes, headings, and emphasis with editorial elegance.
- **Sticky Navigation**: An anchor bar at the bottom provides instant chapter progress, paragraph count, and one-tap / keyboard navigation (`←` and `→`) that stays fixed while you scroll comfortably.

### 🧠 Contextual Text Analysis
Transform passive reading into active learning directly inside the reader view:
- **Explain**: Delve deeper into complex metaphors, historical references, or dense philosophical concepts within the active paragraph.
- **Summarize**: Extract the essence, thesis, or central takeaway of any paragraph in seconds.
- **Vocabulary**: Surface nuanced definitions and context-aware explanations for challenging words.

### 📚 Offline English WordNet Dictionary
- **Zero-Latency Offline Lookup**: Integrated with Princeton WordNet via **Gown**, allowing you to lookup English definitions, parts of speech, and usage examples offline without sending data over the network.
- **Hybrid Intelligence**: Enjoy instant offline vocabulary lookup when on the go, with optional generative AI expansion when connected.

### 🎨 Atmosphere & Ambient Aesthetics
- **Frameless Borderless Window**: Minimalist modern desktop window with sleek integrated drag titlebars and controls.
- **Ambient Lighting System**: Soft, animated radial aura meshes and subtle grid textures that create a calm, cozy reading environment.
- **Refined Glassmorphism**: Translucent floating panels, subtle borders, and smooth lighting accents engineered with Radix Colors alpha tokens.
- **Adaptive Dark & Light Modes**: Seamless contrast transitions tailored for late-night reading sessions and bright daytime environments alike.

### 📚 Smart Library & Catalog
- **One-Click EPUB Import**: Simply select your favorite `.epub` files. Periodus extracts chapters, structures paragraphs, and prepares your book for reading.
- **Automated Cover & Metadata Retrieval**: Automatically matches titles and authors with Open Library to source high-resolution cover artwork.
- **Continue Reading**: Pick up right where you left off with saved reading positions and completion percentages.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Native support for English and Indonesian languages.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Offline Freedom

- **Local-First Architecture**: Your books, reading progress, and dictionary database live exclusively on your computer in local storage.
- **Private AI Configuration**: Bring your own Google Gemini API key. Keys are saved securely in your local database and communicated directly with AI providers without third-party intermediaries.
